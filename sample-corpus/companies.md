# Company summaries

## Quillfeather Systems (Director of Product, 2021–2024)

Quillfeather Systems builds planning and dispatch software for regional fleet operators. Its flagship product is Marrowline, a route planner used by about 140 fleet customers by 2022. While Jordan was there, the company was a mid-sized software business of roughly 150 people, past its early growth and working to make its single product more reliable and better priced. Jordan's product group of six sat inside the product organization and worked day to day with a 22-person engineering and operations team, with the goal of tying those groups to shared targets.

## Gullwing Freight Labs (Senior Product Manager, 2018–2021)

Gullwing Freight Labs is a freight logistics company that combines shipment tracking for its own carrier network with software for drivers and dispatchers. Its best-known consumer-facing product is Beacon, a shipment-tracking app for drivers that reached about 30,000 monthly active users during Jordan's time there. The company was a growth-stage business of roughly 400 employees, with a large field workforce and a smaller central product and data group. Jordan's team sat in that central product group, working closely with the driver app engineers and the analytics team that built the late-delivery reporting.

## Lumenpost Apps (Product Manager, 2015–2018)

Lumenpost Apps is a small consumer software studio, with roughly 60 employees while Jordan was there, that built a handful of mobile apps for small businesses and field teams. Its main product in Jordan's years was Tally, a mobile tool for logging check-ins and job activity. Jordan was a product manager on the Tally team, which was a single product group of about eight people sharing engineering and design resources with the rest of the studio.
