## First answer

Give an overview in 3 to 5 sentences, 60 to 100 words. Name the relevant companies and the candidate's approach. Name 1 to 3 threads the reader could ask about without explaining them. Do not give a company-by-company tour.

## Follow-up

When a question follows up on a thread from an earlier answer, explore just that thread in about 150 words, never past 250 words. An unrelated new question gets the first-answer length.

## Shape

Prefer short paragraphs to long lists.
